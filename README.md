## Project Overview

This repository contains my Digital Literacy Portfolio created as part of the CSE0001 course. The project focuses on understanding and applying essential digital skills such as online safety, professional communication, use of digital platforms, and cyber awareness. It includes five tasks covering infographic design, digital portfolio creation, platform exploration, email etiquette, and cybercrime awareness.

---

## 📂 Tasks Summary

### 🔹 Task 1: Digital Literacy Infographic
Created an infographic using Canva explaining digital literacy, safe internet practices, useful tools, and professional online presence.

### 🔹 Task 2: Digital Portfolio
Set up profiles on GitHub, Stack Overflow, and Kaggle to build a professional digital presence and showcase learning.

### 🔹 Task 3: Platforms Exploration
Completed a coding problem on HackerRank and created a Google Form quiz for digital literacy awareness.

👉 **Google Form Link:** https://forms.gle/aF1tuEPErRKQWXBT6

### 🔹 Task 4: Email Etiquette
Drafted two professional emails and created a checklist of do’s and don’ts for responsible social media usage.

### 🔹 Task 5: Cybercrime Awareness
Prepared a case study on UPI fraud and created a prevention checklist to promote safe online practices.
