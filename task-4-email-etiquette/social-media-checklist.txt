Social Media Do's and Don'ts

DO's

1. *DO use privacy settings* - Set profiles to private and review them monthly

2. *DO think before posting* - Ask "Would I want my professor/parent/employer to see this?"

3. *DO use separate accounts* - Professional vs personal profiles for different purposes

4. *DO verify information* - Check sources before sharing news or articles

5. *DO report harassment* - Use platform tools to report cyberbullying or inappropriate content

 DON'TS 

1. *DON'T share sensitive information* - Never post location, travel plans, or personal IDs online

2. *DON'T engage with trolls* - Responding to negativity escalates situations

3. *DON'T post during emotional moments* - Wait 24 hours before posting when angry or upset

4. *DON'T accept random friend requests* - Verify identity before connecting with strangers

5. *DON'T overshare* - Avoid posting grades, relationship issues, or confidential college matters
