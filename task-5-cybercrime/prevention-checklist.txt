Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

1. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or passwords with anyone.
2. Do not click on unknown links or download files from untrusted sources.
3. Always verify the sender before responding to messages or calls.
4. Do not scan QR codes to receive money; QR codes are used to send money.
5. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on all important accounts.
6. Use strong and unique passwords for different platforms.
7. Regularly update your apps and devices to avoid security risks.
8. Avoid using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions.
9. Always check the URL of websites before entering personal information.
10. Report any suspicious activity immediately.

Reporting:
National Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
Helpline Number: 1930
